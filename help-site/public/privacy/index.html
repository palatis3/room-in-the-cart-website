Privacy policy

Policy date · November 27, 2026

How Venture House Group LLC handles information in Room in the Cart for iPhone and on these support and privacy pages.

How parents and guardians are verified

Your shopping records

Your private lists, pantry inventory, dinners, prices, spending limits, pickup history and any receipt images or notes you save are stored on your iPhone. We do not receive a copy when you save or edit them. Apple’s iCloud Backup may include local app data, depending on your iPhone settings. You can manage those backups in iCloud settings.

You can use private shopping, pantry and dinner planning without an account. Public household sign-up is currently closed. The connected features described below apply only when offered in your app, for your role and region. We do not use advertising identifiers or track your activity across other companies’ apps or websites.

Saving a private receipt draft

If your app offers Save draft on this iPhone, you can choose to keep an unfinished receipt in your private workspace. At line selection, it keeps all recognized text, your edits and selections. At putaway review, it keeps the lines in that review, including skipped ones, and the store, date, amounts and storage choices you entered. The protected draft is excluded from device backups and contains no original photo or file. You can reopen or discard it. Saving a draft does not add food to your pantry. You review and confirm that separately.

When you use a connected household

If account access is available to you, Supabase processes your sign-in email and account identifier and hosts connected records in the United States. When Resend delivers a code, it receives the destination email and the message. Vercel hosts the household service. Authentication and service providers receive connection information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the service.

Joining a household does not upload your private lists. When you send a grocery request, reply, share instructions or record a household shop, we receive the member and household identifiers, your display name and the details you submit. The household members who can see each item depend on their role and the shop. If you choose to record a household receipt, adults with pantry access can see the store, date, any reference, the original wording of selected lines, food, quantities, storage locations and any prices you entered. A code derived from all recognized lines, including skipped lines, helps prevent duplicate entries. The original receipt file stays on your iPhone for this action.

Apple subscriptions and adult access

If the app offers Household Annual, Apple handles billing and shows the price before confirmation. The app and household service use signed purchase evidence, transaction identifiers, an app account token, subscription status and the purchaser, household and named adult seat records to grant or restore paid access and prevent reuse of a purchase. Apple handles payment card and bank details. Deleting your household account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Use Apple Subscriptions to manage or cancel it. The Apple Standard EULA governs the app license.

Annual renewal reminders in Mexico

Venture House Group LLC is not currently sending supplemental renewal emails to customers. If enabled, for an ordinary Mexico annual renewal at an unchanged price, we would use Apple’s signed renewal information to check the renewal status, date, next amount and currency. We would use the verified email of the current paying adult and aim to send a reminder seven days before renewal. Resend would receive the recipient’s email address and the message with the renewal details and a link to manage the subscription with Apple. Other household members would not receive the email. Delivery or bounce information would help us track whether the message reached the recipient’s mail service. Apple manages billing and cancellation.

Children and guardian permission

Where Apple requires an age-range check, the app uses the response on this iPhone to decide whether to open the workspace. It does not send the age range to your household. Child enrollment is a separate feature and is currently closed. The planned app release and child enrollment are limited to the United States and Mexico. KWS offers verification in other countries as well. The methods below depend on the child’s country of residence.

If enrollment becomes available, a parent or guardian reviews the notice and declares their relationship to the child. To request an adult check, the household service sends the adult’s verified email address, language, the child’s country or region, the parent-verification purpose and a reference for this attempt to Epic’s Kids Web Services (KWS).

  • United States. The adult can use a credit or debit card with Stripe, or the last four digits of their Social Security number with Veratad. With Veratad, the adult enters their legal first and last names, date of birth, street address, ZIP code and last four digits.
  • Mexico. The adult can use their own CURP with Nubarium or a debit card with Stripe. For the CURP check, the adult enters their first name, paternal and maternal surnames and 18-character CURP on the KWS form.

For a card check, KWS uses the adult’s IP address to estimate their current country. If the adult is in the United States, a small charge is made and refunded. In Mexico, a small temporary authorization in local currency may appear on the card without money being withdrawn. The provider shows the amount before the adult proceeds. Epic’s guide to parental consent

Enter these details on the verification provider’s form. Room in the Cart does not receive the card number, Social Security digits or CURP. Our service receives the verified adult’s email, signed verification result, time and reference. To recognize adults across participating services, KWS uses a hashed email, verification method and date in its AgeGraph. You can opt out of AgeGraph through the link in the verification confirmation email. KWS verification and privacy information

Verifying adulthood does not give the child access. The guardian must separately grant permission, identify the intended child sign-in email and confirm device pairing. If granted, the service uses the child’s email, alias, paired device or session, enrollment and consent records to provide limited access to their own grocery requests and permitted replies. Their requests are visible to household members authorized to handle them. Paid planning seats are for adults. A verified guardian can withdraw permission in Guardian controls, request deletion of the child’s account, or contact us to request access or correction. If we need to review authority, we keep a case reference, relevant enrollment references, status and dates for that review. Contact us for assistance if a verification method is unavailable. Do not email identity documents or sign-in codes.

We keep evidence of active guardian consent while it is in force. Ended, incomplete setups become eligible for scheduled cleanup 72 hours after their scheduled expiry. After a guardian withdraws consent, the enrollment evidence becomes eligible for cleanup after 30 days. For a private request to review the authority to delete a child’s account, a case matched to a child’s enrollment becomes eligible for cleanup after 30 days. An unmatched case becomes eligible after 7 days. These are eligibility periods for our scheduled cleanup. They do not set the timing of deletion from provider systems or backups.

Reporting and optional features

Where reporting is offered, we receive the reason, the reported item and version, and the reporter’s and known sender’s identifiers. You can send a report without quoting the message, or choose an excerpt for an authorized reviewer. Blocking a sender transmits no message text. The operator’s policy is daily review of new reports and expiry of report evidence and submission receipts 30 days after submission.

If offered, household measurement requires separate consent. We use the reviewed response codes, shop and member references, eligible week, notice version and membership status to study continued use. You can withdraw through Settings. If you choose to share a meal reaction, it can help personalize your dinner suggestions. If you request online wording or food-matching help, the app shows the wording or choices to be sent to the household service and its processing provider, OpenAI. You can type or choose an item yourself.

Connected account deletion

When connected accounts are available, open Household requests in Settings and choose account deletion to review its scope and status. Leaving a household or signing out does not request deletion. For household receipt lines submitted by the deleted account, the service retains duplicate-prevention codes and erased line positions without the printed wording. A limited code derived from the original Apple transaction can prevent a deleted purchase being reused.

Our policy provides for 30 days for completed deletion receipts, 90 days after completion for minimal live records that help prevent a deleted account from returning, and a 30-day maximum for restorable backups containing account or report data. These periods apply to the named records. Separate purchase, recovery, guardian-review and support records serve different purposes. Contact us to ask which records concern you and how long they are kept.

Camera, photos and voice

The app uses the camera or photos you choose to recognize text and barcodes on your iPhone. Apple may download a selected photo from iCloud. Ordinary dictation uses the microphone and on-device speech recognition where supported. You review the result before saving it. You can type instead and change camera, speech or microphone permissions in iPhone Settings.

When you look up a barcode

Scanning works on the iPhone. If you tap Look up this barcode, Open Food Facts receives the selected product number, the app version and our app contact address. Its server also sees your connection’s IP address. It returns product wording and package size for you to review. You can enter those details yourself. Your receipt, photos, spending limit and pantry list are excluded from the request.

Open Food Facts may keep the barcode you looked up together with your IP address in its request logs. Its privacy policy gives a three-year retention period for visitor logs used for security, technical analysis and measuring visits. It does not give a separate retention period for barcode lookups made by other apps. For questions about records it holds, contact Open Food Facts at privacy@openfoodfacts.org.

When you open a recipe or retailer website

If you choose Read recipe for a web address, the iPhone requests the page directly and extracts the recipe on your device. Each website contacted, including a site reached by a redirect, receives its requested page address, any query parameters in that address, your IP address and an identifier for the app’s recipe reader. Each publisher manages its own request logs. You can paste or type a recipe instead.

Opening a retailer website opens the address you chose in your browser. You choose what to share with that retailer and place orders directly with it. The app uses prices you enter or review.

What others can see

If you turn on a Home Screen widget, someone looking at your screen may see counts and a reviewed dinner title. You can turn the summary off. Optional local notifications may appear on the Lock Screen. Choose what iOS displays in notification settings.

With your permission the app can preview incomplete reminder titles from a list you choose. You select which titles to copy. If you save a dinner event to Calendar, the calendar account and anyone with access to that calendar may see the event.

Before sharing a list, exporting a readable record or saving an encrypted recovery file, review the selected items, notes and photos. Your chosen recipient, app or storage provider receives that copy. Keep your recovery key separately.

Keeping and removing records

To review or remove saved records, open Records → Recovery, exports and saved records. Use the deletion option for the source or recovered copy you want to remove. Delete pantry data lets you review the affected pantry records before confirming. To remove the app and its local documents from this iPhone, use Delete App in iPhone settings.

Remove exports, Calendar events and device backups separately where you saved them. Recipients control the copies you already sent.

When you contact us or visit these pages

If you email hello@roominthecart.com, we receive your sender address, message and any attachments you choose. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards them to our Gmail inbox. We use your support correspondence only to respond to your request. Mail remains in the mailbox until it is deleted. The optional support summary is sent only when you choose a destination and share it.

When you open Privacy or Support, your browser connects to Vercel, which hosts these pages. Vercel receives the page address and your IP address and can use the IP address to determine your approximate city and country. It processes technical records to run and secure its service and removes or anonymizes personal information in its service-generated records when it no longer needs it for its stated purposes. These pages contain no analytics scripts or submission forms. Vercel Privacy Notice.

For privacy questions, or to ask about access to or deletion of support correspondence, email hello@roominthecart.com from the address you used. You can describe your request without sending your shopping records.

Cómo trata Venture House Group LLC la información en Room in the Cart para iPhone y en estas páginas de soporte y privacidad.

Cómo se verifica la mayoría de edad de madres, padres y tutores

Tus registros de compras

Tus listas privadas, el inventario de la despensa, las cenas, los precios, los límites de gasto, el historial de pedidos para recoger y las imágenes de recibos o notas que guardes se almacenan en tu iPhone. Al guardarlos o editarlos, no recibimos una copia. Según la configuración de tu iPhone, el respaldo en iCloud de Apple puede incluir datos locales de la app. Puedes gestionar esos respaldos en la configuración de iCloud.

Puedes gestionar tus compras, tu despensa y tus cenas en privado sin crear una cuenta. Por ahora, el registro público de hogares está cerrado. Las funciones conectadas que se describen a continuación solo se aplican cuando la app te las ofrece según tu función en el hogar y tu región. No usamos identificadores publicitarios ni rastreamos tu actividad entre apps o sitios web de otras empresas.

Guardar el borrador de un recibo privado

Si tu app ofrece Guardar borrador en este iPhone, puedes conservar un recibo pendiente en tu espacio privado. Al seleccionar los renglones, se guardan todo el texto reconocido, tus correcciones y tu selección. Al revisar lo que vas a guardar en la despensa, se conservan todos los renglones de esa revisión, incluso los que omitiste, así como la tienda, la fecha, las cantidades y los lugares de almacenamiento que indicaste. El borrador está protegido, se excluye de los respaldos del dispositivo y no contiene la foto ni el archivo original. Puedes retomarlo o descartarlo. Para agregar los alimentos a tu despensa, revisa y confirma lo que vas a guardar.

Cuando usas un hogar conectado

Si tienes acceso a una cuenta, Supabase procesa tu correo de acceso y el identificador de tu cuenta, y aloja los registros conectados en Estados Unidos. Cuando Resend entrega un código, recibe la dirección de correo de destino y el mensaje. Vercel aloja el servicio del hogar. Para prestar y proteger el servicio, estos proveedores reciben información de la conexión, incluida tu dirección IP.

Unirte a un hogar no sube tus listas privadas. Si envías una solicitud de productos, respondes, compartes instrucciones o registras una compra del hogar, recibimos tu nombre visible, los identificadores del miembro y del hogar, y los detalles que envías. Cada integrante puede ver la información que le corresponde según su función y la compra. Si confirmas un recibo del hogar, las personas adultas con acceso a la despensa pueden ver la tienda, la fecha, la referencia que indiques, el texto original de los renglones seleccionados, los alimentos, las cantidades, los lugares de almacenamiento y los precios que ingreses. Un código derivado de todos los renglones reconocidos, incluso los omitidos, ayuda a detectar duplicados. El archivo original del recibo permanece en tu iPhone durante este proceso.

Si la app ofrece la membresía anual del hogar, Apple procesa el pago y muestra el precio antes de que confirmes. Para conceder o restablecer el acceso de pago y evitar que una compra se use de nuevo, la app y el servicio del hogar utilizan el comprobante firmado de la compra, los identificadores de transacción, un código de cuenta de la app, el estado de la suscripción y los registros del comprador, el hogar y los lugares asignados a personas adultas. Apple gestiona los datos de tu tarjeta y de tu cuenta bancaria. Eliminar tu cuenta del hogar no cancela la suscripción de Apple. Puedes administrarla o cancelarla en Suscripciones de Apple. La licencia de la app se rige por el contrato estándar de Apple. Puedes consultarlo en los términos de los servicios multimedia de Apple para México.

Recordatorios de renovación anual en México

Si habilitamos este aviso, para una renovación anual ordinaria en México sin cambio de precio comprobaremos el estado y la fecha de renovación, el próximo importe y la moneda con los datos de renovación firmados por Apple. Usaremos el correo verificado de la persona adulta que paga la suscripción e intentaremos enviarle el recordatorio siete días antes de la renovación. Resend recibirá la dirección de destino y el mensaje con los detalles de la renovación y un enlace para administrar la suscripción con Apple. Los demás integrantes del hogar no recibirán el correo. Menores y permiso de la madre, el padre o el tutor

Cuando Apple requiere comprobar un rango de edad, la app utiliza la respuesta en este iPhone para decidir si abre el espacio de trabajo. No envía el rango de edad al hogar. La inscripción de menores es una función aparte y por ahora está cerrada. El lanzamiento previsto de la app y la inscripción de menores se limitan a Estados Unidos y México. KWS también ofrece verificación en otros países. Los siguientes métodos dependen del país de residencia del menor.

Si se habilita la inscripción, la madre, el padre o el tutor revisa el aviso y declara su relación con el menor. Para solicitar la verificación de mayoría de edad, el servicio del hogar envía a Kids Web Services (KWS) de Epic el correo verificado de la persona adulta, el idioma, el país o región de residencia del menor, el motivo de la verificación y una referencia del trámite.

  • Estados Unidos. La persona adulta podrá usar una tarjeta de crédito o débito con Stripe, o los últimos cuatro dígitos de su número de Seguro Social con Veratad. Con Veratad, deberá ingresar su nombre y apellidos legales, fecha de nacimiento, domicilio, código postal y los últimos cuatro dígitos.
  • México. La persona adulta podrá usar su propia CURP con Nubarium o una tarjeta de débito con Stripe. Para la verificación con CURP, ingresará su nombre, apellidos paterno y materno y su CURP de 18 caracteres en el formulario de KWS.

Para verificar una tarjeta, KWS usa la dirección IP de la persona adulta para estimar en qué país se encuentra. Si está en Estados Unidos, se hace un cargo pequeño que después se reembolsa. En México, puede aparecer una autorización temporal por un importe pequeño en moneda local, sin que se retire dinero de la cuenta. El proveedor muestra el importe antes de continuar. Usamos tus mensajes de soporte únicamente para atender tu solicitud. El correo permanece en el buzón hasta que se elimina. El resumen de soporte opcional se envía solo cuando eliges un destino y lo compartes.

Cuando abres la política de privacidad o la página de soporte, tu navegador se conecta a Vercel, que aloja estas páginas. Vercel recibe la dirección de la página y tu dirección IP. A partir de la IP puede estimar tu ciudad y país. Usa registros técnicos para operar y proteger su servicio. Elimina o anonimiza los datos personales de los registros que genera cuando dejan de ser necesarios para los fines que declara. Estas páginas no contienen herramientas de análisis ni formularios de envío. Aviso de privacidad de Vercel.

Si tienes preguntas sobre privacidad o quieres solicitar acceso a tu correspondencia de soporte o pedir que se elimine, escribe a hello@roominthecart.com desde la dirección que utilizaste. Puedes explicar tu solicitud sin enviarnos tus registros de compras.
